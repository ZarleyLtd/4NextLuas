4NextLuas privacy policy

4NextLuas is an Alexa skill published by Zarley Ltd. It tells you the next trams from a Dublin Luas stop using publicly available data from the National Transport Authority.

What we store

If you set a favourite stop, we store that stop (including direction) against the Amazon account identifier Alexa sends us. We do not receive your name, email address, or home address. We do not use the device location.

What we do not do

The skill does not sell data, show advertising, or share the favourite stop with anyone else. Tram times are fetched from the National Transport Authority and are not stored as a personal travel history.

How to remove your favourite

Disable the skill in the Alexa app, or set a different favourite stop. Disabling the skill stops new requests; you can ask us to delete a stored favourite by emailing the address below.
